How to Play

Gather: select Peasants, then right-click resources.

Repair: select Peasants, then right-click a damaged friendly building.

Build: choose a building, then left-click clear ground.

Houses: each House increases your population limit by 10.

Train: Town Centers make Peasants; Barracks make Warriors, Archers, and Samurai; Stables make Cavalry.

Rally: select a production building, then right-click the map to set its rally point.

Command: select units, then right-click to move or attack.

Mobile: tap units to select, tap a destination, resource, or enemy to command, and drag the map to pan.

Win: destroy all enemy Town Centers.

How to Play

  1. Use Peasants to gather food, wood, gold, and stone.
  2. Place resource buildings to grow your economy, then construct a Barracks or Stable for military units.
  3. Select your units, then right-click a destination, resource, or enemy to command them.
  4. Destroy every enemy Town Center to win. Protect your own Town Center to avoid defeat.
